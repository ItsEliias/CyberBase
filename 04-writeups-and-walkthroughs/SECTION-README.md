## How to use this section

1. **Do the room/machine first** — always attempt it yourself before reading a writeup
2. **Compare your approach** — see what you missed or what could be more efficient
3. **Study the AD walkthroughs** — even if you're not doing the machine, reading through AD attack chains builds your mental model
4. **Model your own reports** from the pentest-reports examples
